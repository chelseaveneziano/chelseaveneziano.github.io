Featured · Current project

Home Price Direction Prediction

Predicts whether home prices will rise or fall the following month across the 15 largest U.S. metro areas. It combines mortgage rate, unemployment, and inflation data from the FRED API with the Zillow Home Value Index. On held out data from 2022 onward, Random Forest reached 88% accuracy and an F1 score of 0.91, well above the logistic regression baseline.

Question: Can economic indicators predict next month's direction of home prices?

Public health · Multi-source

Life Expectancy Trends, 2003 to 2023

Combines three source types: a Kaggle flat file, tables scraped from Wikipedia, and the WHO Global Health Observatory API. Each source is cleaned separately, loaded into SQLite, and joined by country. The results show a lasting gap between developed and developing countries and positive links between life expectancy, schooling, and immunization.

Question: What factors are linked to differences in life expectancy between countries?

Data engineering

Telco Customer Churn Big Data Pipeline

A full pipeline that predicts customer churn. NiFi ingests 7,043 customer records into HDFS, Hive structures them for querying, PySpark MLlib trains a logistic regression model, and the evaluation metrics are written to HBase. The model reached about 80% accuracy and an AUC of 0.84.

Question: Which customers are likely to leave, and how do you build that at scale?

Machine learning · Health

Diabetes Risk Prediction

Flags patients at risk for diabetes using 768 patient records and 8 clinical features. It compares Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost on accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and ROC AUC. Random Forest was the most balanced model at 77% accuracy and 69% recall, with glucose and BMI as the strongest predictors.

Question: Which clinical measures best identify patients at risk for diabetes?

scikit-learnXGBoostRandom Forest77% accuracy

Statistics · Health

Heart Disease EDA and Regression

Analyzes 1,025 heart disease patient records to test whether age, sex, and cholesterol affect resting blood pressure, using distributions, PMF and CDF analysis, correlation, a t test, and OLS regression. Age and cholesterol were significant predictors, but the model explained little of the variance (R² = 0.080).

Question: Do age, sex, and cholesterol affect resting blood pressure?

Netflix Top 10 Longevity Analysis

Cleans and joins three of Netflix's public Top 10 datasets, including more than 270,000 country level weekly rankings, to find the titles with the most cumulative weeks in the global Top 10. The analysis argues that weeks on the list measure staying power better than a strong debut, and presents the findings for a content strategy audience.
