About

Featured · Capstone · DSC 680

Housing Price Direction Prediction

A classification approach to housing markets across 15 U.S. metro areas. Instead of predicting an exact price, the model predicts whether prices will rise or fall, using FRED economic indicators and Zillow home value data. The final Random Forest model reached 88% accuracy, giving buyers, planners, and housing agencies a clearer signal to act on.

Question: Can economic and market indicators reliably predict the direction of housing price movement?
